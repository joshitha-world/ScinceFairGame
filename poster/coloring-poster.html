About my project

For our Edu Fair I built a 60-second quiz game that runs on a tablet. Type your name, pick your level — Easy, Medium, Hard or Mixed — choose a game and answer as many questions as you can before the clock runs out. Every answer shows a quick fact, so you learn even when you get it wrong! At the end, earn up to three stars, snap a photo and print your own keepsake receipt. It even works without the internet.
